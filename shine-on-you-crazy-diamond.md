## Patch 1: "Shine Lead" (mono brass-like lead, right hand)

Assumption: this is the brassy Minimoog lead line, not the pads or organ.

### Minimoog Model D settings (Reverb walkthrough; knob values are on the Minimoog's 0-10 scale)

- Oscillator 1: 8', sawtooth `#sourced`
- Oscillator 2: same settings, optional, to thicken `#sourced`
- Osc 1 and 2 tuned very close so the beating is slow `#unverified` (Gearspace forum poster)
- Filter cutoff: fully counter-clockwise `#sourced`
- Filter emphasis: fully counter-clockwise `#sourced`
- Amount of Contour: 6 `#sourced`
- Filter Modulation: on `#sourced`
- Keyboard Control: second filter switch on (higher notes slightly brighter) `#sourced`
- Filter contour: attack 600 ms, decay 400 ms, sustain about 9 `#sourced`
- Loudness contour: attack 250 ms, decay 1 s, sustain 7 `#sourced`
- Glide: on, decay switch on, glide value 6 `#sourced`
- Oscillator 3 as LFO: Osc 3 Control off, frequency 4, sine wave `#sourced`
- Mod wheel: engages the LFO on the filter, for a horn-like tremolo on accents; use lightly `#sourced`

### Tuning cautions (Gearspace forum poster)

- Too much contour or too low a sustain gives a brassy attack followed by a low sine wave. Keep enough sawtooth harmonics in the sustain. `#unverified`
- A ladder-style filter helps a lot to get close. `#unverified`
