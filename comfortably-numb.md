## What I found

- Richard Wright plays Hammond organ on the track. `#sourced`
- In the second guitar solo, the Hammond is mixed up slightly and the distorted rhythm guitar is mixed down. Listen there for the organ. `#unverified` (blog comment)
- Studio credits conflict: one list gives Wright synthesizer, piano and organ, another only Hammond organ. `#unverified`
- Live show breakdown: Rick on organ, Gilmour covering low Prophet notes in the outro solo. This describes the live show, not necessarily the record. `#unverified`
- The orchestra is Michael Kamen's arrangement, not keyboards. If my backing stem includes it, I don't need to cover it. `#sourced`
- Key: the verses were originally in E minor and moved to B minor at Waters's request. `#sourced`
