## Notes

1. The article gives "6886". I read it as the first four drawbars (16', 5⅓', 8', 4') at 6, 8, 8, 6, rest at zero. The article doesn't spell out that mapping. `#estimated`
2. A forum post suggested 688800000 upper. The article's 6886 supersedes it. Ignore the forum value.
3. Where the numbers come from: a fan account of a 2003 conversation between the article's author and Matthew Fisher, recalled from memory. Not independently confirmed.
4. The article's photos show Fisher's own 1963 C3, not necessarily the organ used on the 1967 record. Other sources say M100 or M-102. `#unverified`
